 Susu Web App
Simple. Transparent. Accountable.
Susu Web App is a modern, web-based platform designed to digitize and simplify the management of Susu groups (Rotating Savings and Credit Associations). Built with React and MySQL, it replaces error-prone paper records with a secure, automated system that ensures every member gets their fair turn.
🌟 Why It’s Unique
Unlike standard tracking apps, Susu Web App is built for real-world usability:
"Next-in-Line" Logic: The system automatically calculates who is eligible to "eat" the pot next, preventing arguments and ensuring fairness.
Offline-First Design: Works even with poor internet connectivity, syncing data to the cloud once the connection is restored.
Trust & Transparency: Features an immutable Audit Log that tracks every payment and change, building trust within the group.
Accessible UI: Designed with large buttons, clear color-coding (✅/❌), and plain language so anyone, regardless of tech skills, can use it easily.
🚀 Key Features
Member Management: Easy registration with unique usernames and roles (Admin/Member).
Smart Payment Tracking: Record cash or proxy payments with a single click.
Automated Cycles: Tracks weekly contributions and automatically resets when a member collects the pot.
Visual Dashboard: Admins see a real-time overview of who has paid, who is pending, and who is next to collect.
Accountability Reports: Generate clear history logs of all payments and collections.
🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React.js (Responsive, PWA-ready)
Backend: Node.js / Express
Database: MySQL
Security: Bcrypt for password hashing, JWT for authentication
🎯 Objectives
Automate the tedious process of manual Susu tracking.
Reduce errors and lost records associated with paper notebooks.
Provide total transparency for both admins and members.
Ensure accountability through detailed digital logs.
